# Closure and Verification Records

A closure record is the evidence gate for a milestone.

Layout:

plans/closure/<subsystem>/NNN-status.md

Required contents include source plan/roadmap, reviewed baseline, implementation commits, executive finding, requirement-to-evidence matrix, exact commands/outcomes, invariant review, failure/recovery review, compatibility review, security/anonymity review, documentation/operations, unresolved findings, roadmap disposition, and registry updates.

Allowed statuses are closed, conditionally closed, corrective pass required, and blocked.

Compilation or formatting alone cannot close a milestone. Required security, restart, fault, or capability evidence may not be silently omitted.
