## SV-001 | CASEMAPPING ascii folds only ASCII case
spec: IRCv3 ISUPPORT CASEMAPPING (ascii)
status: stable
utf8: relevant
why: Nick and channel comparison must follow the mapping the server advertises.
line: :srv 005 bot CASEMAPPING=ascii\r\n
nick_same: Bot,bot=true
nick_same: nick[,nick{=false
nick_same: nick^,nick~=false

## SV-002 | CASEMAPPING rfc1459 folds the defined character classes
spec: RFC 1459 casemapping (`{}`, `|`, and `^~` are case pairs); IRCv3 ISUPPORT CASEMAPPING (rfc1459)
status: stable
utf8: relevant
why: The default mapping folds three specific character pairs; a bouncer comparing nicks naively gets this wrong.
nick_same: Bot,bot=true
nick_same: nick[,nick{=true
nick_same: nick],nick}=true
nick_same: nick^,nick~=true
nick_same: nick[o[,nick{o[=true
nick_same: nick[o\,nick[o|=true

## SV-003 | rfc1459-strict stops folding the tilde
spec: Modern IRC Client Protocol, RPL_ISUPPORT CASEMAPPING parameter (rfc1459-strict)
status: stable
utf8: relevant
why: With rfc1459-strict `bot~` and `bot^` are different nicks; folding them would merge identities.
line: :srv 005 bot CASEMAPPING=rfc1459-strict\r\n
nick_same: bot~,bot^=false
nick_same: nick[,nick{=true
nick_same: Bot,bot=true
