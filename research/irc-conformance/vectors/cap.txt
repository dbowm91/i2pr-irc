## CV-001 | NICK and USER with no CAP register immediately
spec: RFC 2812 section 3.1 (NICK, USER), section 4.1.1 (numeric 001)
status: stable
utf8: relevant
why: The baseline for ordinary clients must not regress while CAP is implemented.
client: NICK bot\r\n
check: after the preceding client line
expect: ready=false
expect: negotiating=false
client: USER bot 0 * :phone\r\n
check: after the preceding client line
expect: ready=true
expect: welcome_count=1

## CV-006 | The bouncer advertises only implemented downstream capabilities
spec: IRCv3 capability negotiation (stable)
status: stable
utf8: relevant
why: Advertising an unimplemented capability produces client-visible failures later. The served set is the history drafts plus the tag surface; anything not served must stay out of `CAP LS`.
client: CAP LS 302\r\n
check: after the preceding client line
expect: contains=CAP * LS :
expect: contains=message-tags
expect: contains=labeled-response
expect: absent=sasl
expect: absent=echo-message
expect: absent=away-notify
