## WF-001 | A line split across three chunks is decoded once
spec: RFC 2812 section 2.3; incremental stream framing
status: stable
utf8: irrelevant
why: A bouncer reads a live stream, so partial frames are the normal case.
chunks: PIN|G :hel|lo\r\n
lines: 1
line: PING :hello

## WF-002 | Two complete lines in one chunk are both delivered
spec: RFC 2812 section 2.3
status: stable
utf8: irrelevant
why: Coalesced reads must not lose the second message.
chunks: PING :a\r\nPING :b\r\n

line: PING :b
